# Gilded Pages

Create a premium cinematic hero animation for my Library Catalog & Recommendations web application.

The hero animation should feel like a magical, sophisticated library — inspired by the visual style of a cinematic fantasy library, but designed specifically for a modern premium library platform.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT create a generic SaaS animation, abstract blobs, floating UI cards, or random 3D shapes.

The animation must be entirely centered around BOOKS, READING, and a LIBRARY.

HERO CONCEPT:

"Discover your next chapter"

The hero should show a grand, atmospheric library in the background with tall bookshelves filled with hundreds of books. Warm golden light should enter through large library windows, with subtle dust particles and cinematic depth of field.

Animation sequence:

0–1 seconds:

- Start with the grand library environment.

- Several books gently float/fall through the air.

- Some books are closed and some are naturally open.

- Movement should be slow, elegant and realistic.

- Background bookshelves remain mostly static.

1–2 seconds:

- One beautiful hardcover book from the background slowly moves toward the center of the hero.

- The book should rotate very slightly in 3D as it approaches.

- Other floating books continue moving subtly in the background.

2–3 seconds:

- The central book slowly opens.

- Warm golden light softly comes from between its pages.

- Pages move naturally, not aggressively.

- Add subtle floating dust/sparkle particles around the book.

3–5 seconds:

- Camera gently moves closer to the open book.

- The pages become the visual focus.

- Elegant typography appears naturally on the open pages:

"𝐃𝐈𝐒𝐂𝐎𝐕𝐄𝐑 𝐘𝐎𝐔𝐑 𝐍𝐄𝐖 𝐂𝐇𝐀𝐏𝐓𝐄𝐑"

The typography should look like premium literary/editorial typography — elegant serif lettering, warm ivory/gold tone, subtle glow.

5–7 seconds:

- The camera slowly pulls back.

- The book begins closing.

- A soft golden sparkle/light trail surrounds the book as it closes.

- The title remains visible briefly and then fades away.

7–10 seconds:

- The book gently floats backward toward the library shelves.

- Other books continue floating subtly.

- The scene returns to the original library composition.

- The animation should seamlessly loop back to the beginning.

VISUAL STYLE:

- Premium cinematic library

- Warm dark brown / charcoal / deep navy environment

- Warm amber and golden lighting

- Elegant ivory typography

- Realistic hardcover books

- Subtle volumetric light

- Soft dust particles

- Cinematic depth of field

- High-end editorial / luxury publishing aesthetic

- Sophisticated, calm and immersive

- No cartoon styling

- No excessive particle effects

- No neon colors

HERO CONTENT:

Left side of the hero:

Small eyebrow:

"A WORLD OF STORIES AWAITS"

Main headline:

"Discover your next chapter"

Supporting text:

"Explore thousands of books, find new perspectives and let great stories find you."

Primary CTA:

"Explore Catalog →"

Secondary CTA:

"Watch Story"

Below the CTAs show three small statistics:

"10K+ Books"

"5K+ Members"

"20+ Categories"

Right side:

The cinematic library animation should occupy most of the visual area, with the central floating/opening book acting as the focal point.

IMPORTANT UI REQUIREMENT:

The text and buttons must remain readable and stable while the animation happens behind/around them.

Do not let floating books cover the headline, buttons, or important UI.

The animation should enhance the hero rather than make the interface difficult to use.

ANIMATION QUALITY:

- Smooth 60fps-style motion

- Gentle easing

- No sudden jumps

- No excessive camera movement

- No distracting looping

- Seamless loop

- Subtle parallax between foreground books, central book, and background shelves

- Books should have realistic shadows and lighting

- The animation should feel like one continuous cinematic scene rather than separate effects.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN:

Desktop:

Use the full cinematic library scene.

Tablet:

Reduce the number of floating books and keep the central book prominent.

Mobile:

Use a cropped version of the library scene with fewer floating books.

Keep the central book and "Discover your next chapter" message clearly visible.

Do not allow the animation to dominate the entire mobile viewport.

PERFORMANCE:

Implement this efficiently.

Do not create hundreds of expensive DOM elements.

Prefer CSS transforms, opacity, GPU-accelerated animations, or a lightweight canvas/WebGL approach where appropriate.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion and provide a static fallback image for users who disable animation.

OVERALL FEEL:

When someone opens the website, it should feel like they have stepped into a beautiful digital library.

The emotional progression should be:

LIBRARY → BOOK → OPEN → DISCOVER → CLOSE → RETURN

The hero should communicate:

"Every book is a new chapter waiting to be discovered."
